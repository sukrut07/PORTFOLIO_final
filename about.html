How I think about engineering

I approach software engineering from first principles: identify a high-friction problem, design clean architecture, train or configure appropriate models, and deploy reliable user-facing interfaces.

Rather than treating AI and full-stack development as disconnected silos, I combine them: backend systems feed real-time feature stores, machine learning models output calibrated predictions, and responsive clients present explainable insights.

Operating Principles

Build, measure, refine, explain.

  • Learn by shipping: Test ideas through functioning prototypes, hackathons, and open repos.
  • Architect for clarity: Prioritize clean separation of concerns, robust APIs, and deterministic schemas.
  • Honest metrics: Verify ML performance through proper validation without fabricated claims or inflated accuracy numbers.
  • End-to-end perspective: Connect model inference pipelines with production-grade web interfaces and real databases.
